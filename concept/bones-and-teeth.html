Foundation 05

Bones & Teeth

The foundation of your body's structure

Your bones provide structural support, protect vital organs and work together with muscles to enable movement. Your teeth rely on mineralised structures to maintain their strength and function. Maintaining normal bone and dental health involves several nutrients, including calcium, vitamin D, vitamin K, magnesium, phosphorus and zinc.

The science

What does science say?

Research spotlight

Effect of calcium and vitamin D supplementation on bone mineral density in women aged 65–71 years: a 3-year randomized population-based trial (OSTPRE-FPS).

Kärkkäinen et al. · Osteoporosis International · 2010

593Participants
3 yearsStudy duration
800 IUVitamin D3 daily
1,000 mgElemental calcium daily

What did researchers find?

In the intention-to-treat analysis, total-body bone mineral density increased by 0.84% in the supplementation group compared with 0.19% in the control group. The difference was statistically significant. However, differences at several individual skeletal sites, including the lumbar spine and femoral neck, were not statistically significant.

What this research means for HLTHX

This trial provides human research evidence on the relationship between calcium and vitamin D supplementation and bone mineral density in older women. HLTHX contains vitamin D3 at 600 IU per sachet, compared with 800 IU in the study. Its calcium compound quantities are known, but the elemental calcium amount still needs manufacturer confirmation.

The study supports the scientific relevance of calcium and vitamin D to bone health. It does not prove that HLTHX produces the same results.
