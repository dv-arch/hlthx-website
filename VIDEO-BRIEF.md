# HLTHX™ ritual film: "Back from the run"

A 15–20 second film for the home page ("The daily ritual" section) and Instagram.
Someone comes home from a morning run, tears open an HLTHX sachet, pours it into water, it turns golden, they drink.

## Look and feel
- **Real, not glossy.** Natural morning daylight from a window, a bright modern Indian home, real skin with a light sheen of sweat.
- **Camera:** handheld but steady, 35 mm, shallow depth of field, close-ups for the product moments.
- **Colour:** warm whites and soft greys (like the pack), with the **turmeric-gold drink** as the one strong colour.
- **No on-screen text** in the generated shots. Titles and the logo are added in the edit.

## Shots
| # | Length | Shot | Website step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 s | Front door opens; a runner (25–35, athletic wear, earbuds) steps in, breathing, wipes their forehead. | — |
| 2 | 3 s | Kitchen counter, close-up: their hand picks up a **white-and-silver stick sachet** and tears the top strip off. | Open |
| 3 | 4 s | Slow-motion macro: **golden turmeric-coloured powder** pours into a tall clear glass of water and clouds through it. | Pour |
| 4 | 3 s | A spoon stirs; the water turns an even **warm golden** colour. | Mix |
| 5 | 3 s | They drink, lower the glass, exhale, small smile. | Enjoy |
| 6 | 2 s | End frame: the golden glass on the counter, the sachet beside it, soft light. Logo added in the edit. | Enjoy |

## Prompts for an AI video tool (Kling, Veo, Sora, Seedance)
Generate each shot separately, then join them. Use **image-to-video** for shots 2 and 6 with a **real photo of the HLTHX sachet** as
the starting frame. AI tools cannot draw the HLTHX wordmark correctly on their own and will invent a different pack.

1. *Morning, bright modern Indian apartment. A fit runner in their late 20s in a grey running top opens the front door and steps inside, slightly out of breath, light sweat, wipes forehead with the back of the hand. Natural window light, handheld 35 mm, shallow depth of field, realistic, documentary style.*
2. *Close-up on a white marble kitchen counter. A hand picks up a slim white-and-silver stick sachet and tears the top strip off cleanly. Soft morning daylight, macro detail, realistic.* (start frame: real sachet photo)
3. *Slow-motion macro: fine golden turmeric-coloured powder pours from a torn stick sachet into a tall clear glass of still water; golden clouds billow and swirl through the water. Clean white background, soft daylight, photorealistic.*
4. *A metal spoon stirs a tall clear glass; the water turns an even warm golden-yellow colour, a few tiny bubbles. Close-up, soft daylight, photorealistic.*
5. *The runner lifts the glass of golden drink and drinks, lowers it, exhales and smiles slightly, relaxed. Kitchen, morning light, handheld 35 mm, shallow depth of field, realistic.*
6. *A tall glass of warm golden drink on a white kitchen counter beside a white-and-silver stick sachet, morning light, still shot, photorealistic.* (start frame: real sachet photo)

**Avoid (negative prompt):** extra fingers, warped hands, text, logos, labels, watermarks, neon, glow, cartoon, CGI look, over-saturated colour.

## Delivery for the website
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264), 1080 × 1350 (4:5, best for the section) or 1080 × 1920 (9:16, Instagram).
- **Length:** 15–20 s, seamless if it loops. **No sound needed** (it plays muted).
- **Size:** under 8 MB.
- **Upload:** Online Store → Themes → Customize → home page → **Daily ritual (animated)** → *Ritual video*.
  It replaces the drawn animation straight away.
- **Step timing:** in the same section, set each step's *lights up at (seconds)* to the moment it starts in the film.
  For example: Open at 3, Pour at 6, Mix at 10, Enjoy at 13. Leave them empty to split the film evenly.

## Before it goes live
- Use the **real HLTHX sachet** in shots 2 and 6 (image-to-video from a product photo, or a real shoot).
- Check that the drink colour matches the real product once mixed.
- If real people are filmed, get a signed model release.
