# HLTHX™ website: details we need from you

The website is live with these items hidden for now. Once you send them, we add them and they appear on the site.
"Where it shows" is the part of the website that will use it.

## 1. Product and pricing
- [ ] **Product photos**: the box, a single sachet, the drink made up, and any lifestyle photos (high resolution, plain background for the packshots). *Where it shows: home page, product page, pop-up.*
- [ ] **Price of one 30-Day Box** (and a crossed-out "was" price, if any). *Product page, buy box.*
- [ ] **Subscription prices**: monthly, and 3-month (or the discount % for each). *Buy box plan options.*
- [ ] **Launch offer** for the black bar at the top of every page (e.g. "Save 15% on your first box"). *Announcement bar.*
- [ ] **Box contents**: confirm "30 × 15 g sachets" and anything else in the box (scoop, leaflet). *"What's in the box".*
- [ ] **Goal tags**: confirm or change: Daily Nutrition, Energy, Focus, Hydration, Gut Health, Immunity. *Product page tags.*

## 2. Formula
- [ ] **Full ingredient list with the amount of each per 15 g sachet** (the supplement facts table). *"Supplement facts" panel.*
- [ ] **Ingredients in each of the 9 categories**: Multivitamins; Minerals & Trace Elements; Amino Acids; Hydration Electrolytes; Prebiotic Fibre; Probiotic Complex; Digestive Enzyme Support; Heart Health Support; Joint & Muscle Health Support. *"Inside the Formula" tiles.*
- [ ] **Confirm the category list is complete** (add or remove any). *"Inside the Formula".*

## 3. How to use and safety
- [ ] **Directions**: how much water, best time of day, hot or cold. *"Open. Mix. Enjoy." and Directions box.*
- [ ] **Warnings and storage**: who should not take it, storage conditions, shelf life once opened. *Directions box, FAQ.*

## 4. Quality and trust
- [ ] **Manufacturing standards**: facility name, GMP certificate, **FSSAI licence number**. *Quality section.*
- [ ] **Third-party test reports / certificate of analysis** (PDF), and confirmation we can say "Third-party tested". *Quality section, trust strip.*
- [ ] **Ingredient specifications** document (form, source, amount). *Quality section.*
- [ ] **Quality documentation**: storage, shelf life, safety information. *Quality section.*

## 5. The people behind it
- [ ] **Experts who developed or reviewed the formula**: name, credentials, role, and a photo for each (2 or 3 people). *"The science" section, hidden until provided.*

## 6. Reviews
